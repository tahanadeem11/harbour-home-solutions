Service 01

Home Environmental Reviews

We look at your household's structural layout to identify how architectural design and physical barriers impact daily home navigation. Our independent reviews give families a clear, unbiased picture of their home's environment.

  • Independent, structured walkthrough of the entire home
  • Identification of architectural design and physical barriers
  • Clear, easy-to-understand written summary of findings

Service 02

Home Safety Layout Reviews

A Home Safety Layout Review helps identify property-based safety concerns, environmental tripping hazards, and structural barriers within the home, providing practical, universal modification recommendations tailored to your household.

  • Environmental tripping hazard identification
  • Property-based safety concern assessment
  • Practical, universal modification recommendations

Service 03

Aging-in-Place Layout Consulting

Planning to stay in your home for the long term? We provide independent aging-in-place layout consulting to help families understand how their home's layout supports daily living as needs change over time.

  • Layout review focused on long-term daily living needs
  • Guidance matched to standard accessibility guidelines
  • Informed, family-centered recommendations

Service 04

Accessibility & Mobility Assessment

We assess how well a home's physical layout accommodates mobility needs, reviewing doorways, walkways, and room transitions against standard accessibility guidelines to highlight where a home may fall short.

  • Doorway, hallway, and walkway clearance review
  • Room-to-room transition and threshold assessment
  • Comparison against standard accessibility guidelines
Request a Home Review
Service 05

Universal Modification Recommendations

Once concerns are identified, we provide practical universal modification recommendations designed to work for every member of the household, not just a single need, helping you make informed, cost-effective decisions.

  • Universal design recommendations for every household member
  • Prioritized, practical modification guidance
  • Written report to support informed decision-making

Service 06

Post-Hospital / Post-Rehabilitation Home Reviews

These structural reviews are especially helpful when families are preparing a property for a family member's return home after a hospital or rehabilitation stay. We review the home ahead of time so the transition home is safer and better prepared.

  • Pre-discharge home layout review
  • Identification of immediate safety concerns before return home
  • Practical recommendations for a safer transition home

Long-Term Home Modification Planning
